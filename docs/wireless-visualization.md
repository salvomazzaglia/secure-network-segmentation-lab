# Wireless visualization

Wireless associations use synthetic RSSI and **signal buckets**: strong, good, moderate, weak.

## Rules

- Placement is illustrative relative to access points
- No distance-in-meters inference
- No triangulation
- No floor-plan localization in v1.0
- SSIDs are synthetic (`SYNTHETIC-*`)

Signal quality may influence illustrative offset from an AP for teaching, never geolocation.
